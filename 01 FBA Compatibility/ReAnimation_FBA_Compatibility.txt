ReAnimation - Full Body Awareness compatibility

Replaces ReAnimation's 1st-person movement animations with versions whose lower body
(legs, pelvis, root motion) comes from the 3rd-person animations FBA plays in 1st person,
stepping in sync with ReAnimation's upper body.

For correct hands also install "FBA 1st-Person Hands": FBA replaces the 1st-person hand, glove and
gauntlet meshes with 3rd-person ones made for a different finger rig (twisted thumbs); that package
puts the vanilla 1st-person ones back and converts FBA's own animations to their fingers.

Install: add this folder as a data directory AFTER ReAnimation (and after FBA). Only use it
together with OpenMW Full Body Awareness.

Camera tweaks for a visible body (look-down camera offset, hand tilt limit) live in
Dynamic Camera's settings, under Full Body Awareness Tweaks.

The presence of this file is also what tells ReAnimation's scripts that it is installed.
